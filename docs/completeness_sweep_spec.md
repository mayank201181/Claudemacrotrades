COMPLETENESS SWEEP SPEC — Macro Takeaways (both pipelines; read at STEP 2 item 1b; added 9 Oct 2026 at Mayank's request)

PURPOSE. Every feed is someone else's summary, and four of the five lean on the same Grok scanners. This sweep is the digest's own independent check of what actually happened in the last 24h, so a missed or failed feed never means a missed event. It runs every day, after the TAPE CHECK and before the BREADTH SWEEP (STEP 2 item 8).

WINDOW. From 07:00 SGT yesterday to build time today. Note in the footer if you could not cover it.

A. CHECKLIST — web-search each line once (about 6–10 searches in all; combine lines where one search covers them). Prefer primary sources (central-bank sites, statistics offices, TreasuryDirect/MOF/Finanzagentur, OPEC, exchange data), else Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, Nikkei, WSJ.
1. Central banks: any rate decision, minutes, or statement in the window (Fed, ECB, BoJ, BoE, PBoC, RBA, RBNZ, BoC, SNB, Norges, Riksbank, BoK, RBI, MAS, BI, BSP, BoT), plus remarks by the chair/governor or a voting member that moved pricing.
2. Tier-1 data released in the window versus consensus: US, euro area/Germany, UK, Japan, China, Australia; plus Korea, India and Singapore when released. Note any large surprise or revision.
3. Government bond auctions: US Treasury coupon auctions (tail = high yield minus when-issued, per STEP 3D), JGB, Bund, Gilt — flag any weak or strong result.
4. Energy: OPEC+ decisions or source stories, EIA/API inventories, major supply outages, shipping or Gulf/Red Sea incidents.
5. Geopolitics and policy: tariffs and trade actions, sanctions, conflict escalation, elections, fiscal or debt-ceiling news, FX intervention or verbal warnings (MOF Japan, PBoC fixing signals, Korea).
6. Biggest cross-asset moves: from the TAPE CHECK, any instrument whose 24h move looks outsized — find the cause.
7. ASIA-MORNING GAP: the Grok scanners close by ~06:40 SGT. Search what happened since: Asia data versus consensus (Japan 07:50 SGT, Australia, NZ), central-bank news, and how JGBs, USDJPY, KOSPI, AUD and NZD opened, plus any major headline.

B. RECONCILE. For each item found, check (by script over the saved feed texts) whether any feed already carries it.
- Covered: nothing to do; it is evidence like any other item.
- Missed and material (it moves a live question, a theme, or a level he would trade): add it where it belongs — THE LAST 24H IN 60 SECONDS, a theme, the RADAR or the LEDGER — with its SGT time and a primary-source link, prefixed "[sweep]". It goes through the PLAUSIBILITY GATE, the ACCURACY GATE and THE TEST like any other item; a sweep item is independent evidence, not echo.
- Missed and immaterial: drop it silently.
- If a feed disagrees with what the primary source says, verify and correct per STEP 2 item 3.

C. FEED FAILURE. If the Grok feed is missing, or fewer than 3 feeds arrived, the sweep becomes the main news source: widen each checklist line (two searches where needed, up to about 15 in total) and say so in the TOP LINE's coloured span ("Grok feed missing — news from completeness sweep").

D. LIMITS. The sweep adds facts, not opinions: no stances and no voices come from it. It never fabricates a link, and never prints a number without a source it can quote. It does not lengthen the email past the existing word targets — a sweep item displaces a weaker one.

E. FOOTER. One line: "Completeness sweep: K searches · M items found · A added ([sweep]) · window <from>–<to> SGT", or "Completeness sweep: not run — <reason>".
